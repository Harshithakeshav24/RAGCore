RAGCore - Intelligent Enterprise Knowledge Retrieval and Generation System

RAGCore is an intelligent enterprise knowledge retrieval and generation system. It allows users to search for information from enterprise documents and receive relevant, source-grounded answers.

Technologies used by RAGCore include:

- Document processing
- Text extraction
- Embeddings
- Vector search
- Retrieval
- Large language models

The goal of RAGCore is to provide accurate answers based on the information available in uploaded documents.
